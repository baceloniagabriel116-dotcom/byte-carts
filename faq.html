Ordering & Purchases
Browse the All Products page, click a product you like, choose the quantity, and press Add to Cart or Buy Now. From your cart, click Proceed to Checkout, fill in your shipping details, choose a payment method, and complete the purchase.

After completing your purchase you'll be taken to an Order Confirmed page showing your order number, the items you bought, the total, and your estimated delivery date. You can also review all your past orders in your account page.
Payments

Shipping & Delivery
Standard delivery takes approximately 3 business days from the date of purchase. Your exact estimated delivery date is shown on the order confirmation page right after you buy.
Shipping is FREE nationwide on all orders, and no VAT is added to your order total.
Returns & Support
We offer 7-day easy returns. If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact our support team within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement or refund of your store credit.
